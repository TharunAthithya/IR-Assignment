TERMINOLOGY EXPLANATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Vocabulary Size:          The number of unique terms (distinct words)
                          after tokenization across all documents.

Document Frequency (df):  The number of distinct documents that contain
                          a particular term. A measure of how widely a
                          term is distributed across the collection.

Collection Frequency (cf): The total number of times a term appears
                          across all documents in the collection,
                          counting every occurrence.

Positional Occurrences:   The total count of all individual position
                          entries across all terms and documents. Equal
                          to the sum of cf for all terms, and also equal
                          to the total number of tokens in the corpus.
